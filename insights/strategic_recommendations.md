# Strategic Recommendations

The following recommendations translate the analytical findings into actionable business opportunities.

## 1. Optimize Organic Growth

Continue investing in SEO and referral partnerships while monitoring underperforming paid channels for efficiency improvements.

**Why it matters:**  
Organic Search and Referral generated the majority of ecommerce revenue, making these channels important contributors to business performance.

---

## 2. Reduce Checkout Friction

Review payment methods, simplify checkout steps, and conduct usability testing to reduce abandonment between Shipping and Payment.

**Why it matters:**  
The largest observed checkout drop-off occurred between Shipping Information and Payment, representing the clearest conversion optimization opportunity.

---

## 3. Scale High-performing Landing Pages

Increase marketing traffic to the highest-converting landing pages and replicate successful design patterns across lower-performing pages.

**Why it matters:**  
Some landing pages generated significantly stronger conversion and revenue performance despite receiving relatively lower traffic.

---

## 4. Adopt Continuous KPI Monitoring

Regularly monitor acquisition, funnel performance, checkout behavior, and landing page effectiveness using GA4 dashboards to support ongoing optimization.

**Why it matters:**  
Continuous monitoring allows the business to identify emerging performance changes and optimize acquisition and conversion activities over time.
