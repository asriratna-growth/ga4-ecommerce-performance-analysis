# Dashboard Guide

This document provides an overview of the pages and analytical purpose of the GA4 Ecommerce Performance Dashboard.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Project Overview

Provides the context for the analysis, including:

- Business objective
- Dataset information
- Dashboard scope
- Business questions
- Key metrics
- Tools and technology

### 2. Executive Summary

Provides a high-level view of overall ecommerce performance.

Key areas include:

- Users
- Sessions
- Purchases
- Purchase revenue
- Conversion performance
- Revenue efficiency

This page is designed to provide a quick understanding of overall business performance.

### 3. Acquisition Performance

Evaluates how users are acquired through different marketing and traffic channels.

The analysis focuses on:

- Acquired users
- Sessions
- Purchasing users
- Revenue
- Channel performance
- Acquisition efficiency

### 4. Conversion Funnel

Evaluates customer progression through the ecommerce purchase journey.

The funnel analyzes key stages including:

`View Item → Add to Cart → Begin Checkout → Purchase`

The page identifies major drop-off points and opportunities to improve conversion.

### 5. Checkout Performance

Evaluates user progression through the checkout process.

The analysis covers:

`Checkout → Shipping → Payment → Purchase`

Performance is also evaluated by device to identify potential checkout friction.

### 6. Landing Page Effectiveness

Evaluates the performance of landing pages that initiate customer sessions.

Key areas include:

- Sessions
- Purchases
- Conversion performance
- Revenue
- Landing page contribution

### 7. Performance Trends

Analyzes ecommerce performance over time.

The page evaluates trends in:

- Users
- Sessions
- Purchases
- Revenue
- Conversion performance

### 8. Business Insights

Summarizes the key findings identified from the analysis.

Insights are translated from dashboard metrics into business implications and opportunities.

### 9. Strategic Recommendations

Converts the analytical findings into recommended business actions.

Recommendations focus on opportunities related to:

- Customer acquisition
- Conversion optimization
- Checkout experience
- Landing page performance
- Revenue growth

## Dashboard Navigation

The dashboard follows a progression from business context to detailed analysis:

`Overview → Performance → Acquisition → Funnel → Checkout → Landing Pages → Trends → Insights → Recommendations`

This structure allows users to move from understanding the business situation to identifying opportunities and recommended actions.
